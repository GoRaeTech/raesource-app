Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026 · RaeTech LLC

RaeSource is a business-to-business service operated by RaeTech LLC (“we”). It turns published building-permit records into timed sales leads for contractors and suppliers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

We do not sell personal information, we do not serve advertising, and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics in the RaeSource app.

1. Two kinds of information

It matters which is which, so we keep them separate throughout this policy.

a. Information about you, our customer

WhatWhy
Work email addressTo identify your seat and let you sign in
Name and roleSo your colleagues can see who logged a call
Password (stored only as a cryptographic hash) or, if you use Google sign-in, your Google account identifierTo authenticate you
Company, trade, territory and billing status To decide which leads your seat is entitled to
What you record against a lead — notes, stage, contact details you capture, bid valuesTo provide the service, and to share it with the other seats at your company

b. Information about third parties, from public records

The leads themselves come from building permits published as open data by the City of Austin and other Texas jurisdictions. A permit record names the contractor or company that pulled it and, commonly, a business telephone number and the job address. That information is published by the issuing authority as a public record; we compile, classify and time it, we do not create it.

We do not knowingly collect residential contact details of private individuals, and we exclude jurisdictions whose feeds expose personal data such as applicant email addresses or tax identifiers.

2. If you are a contractor named in a permit

You did not sign up for RaeSource, and you may be reading this because somebody contacted you using it. Two things you can ask for:

  • Do not contact. Write to support@goraetech.com and we will flag your company across every customer account so that our software marks you do-not-call.
  • Correction. If we hold something inaccurate about your business, tell us and we will correct or remove it.

We cannot remove the underlying permit record — that belongs to the issuing authority, and you would need to approach them directly.

4. Payments

Card and bank details are collected and held by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1. We never see, receive or store your card number or bank account number. We see only whether a subscription is paid.

5. Security

  • Traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS) and data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.
  • Every table enforces row-level security keyed to your company. It is not that we choose not to show you another customer’s leads — the database will not return them.
  • We do not store passwords. Authentication is handled by Supabase, which stores a one-way hash.
  • Administrative credentials are held outside the application and are never shipped to a browser.

What we do not claim. RaeTech LLC does not currently hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestation. Our infrastructure providers maintain their own certifications, which cover their platforms and not our company. We would rather say that plainly than let the distinction blur.

6. How long we keep things

  • Your account and the work you record — for as long as your company has an active seat, and for 90 days afterwards so that a lapsed account can be restored intact. After that it is deleted.
  • Lead records — refreshed continuously; older permits age out.
  • Do-not-contact requests — kept for five years, because the only way to honour a suppression request is to remember it.
  • Billing records — kept as required by tax and accounting law.

7. Your choices

Write to support@goraetech.com to access, correct, export or delete information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days. If your company holds the account, we may need to confirm the request with its owner before deleting shared work such as call history.

8. Children

RaeSource is a tool sold to businesses. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will email the address on your account before it takes effect.
